## About the Conference

ICETAQC 2027 is an international conference focused on emerging technologies and interdisciplinary research in Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, Cybersecurity, and related areas of computing and engineering.

The conference provides a platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, research scholars, and students to present innovative research, exchange ideas, and discuss emerging technological developments.

---

## Conference Theme

**Emerging Technologies for Intelligent, Secure and Sustainable Computing**

The conference brings together research and applications across:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Computer Vision and Image Processing
- Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Quantum Computing and Quantum Information
- Cybersecurity and Privacy
- Internet of Things and Edge Computing
- Cloud and Distributed Computing
- Data Science and Intelligent Systems
- Emerging Computing Technologies
